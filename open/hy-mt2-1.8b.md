# Tencent Hy-MT2-1.8B

Compact 1.8B-parameter multilingual translation model from Tencent, released alongside the larger Hy-MT2-30B-A3B on Aug 20, 2026. Supports 33 language pairs plus 5 Chinese dialect and minority-language pairs.
