## Overview

Claude Opus 5 is Anthropic's new flagship model, released July 24, 2026 — the fourth Claude 5 model in under two months. Anthropic positions Opus 5 as its everyday model for enterprises, knowledge workers, and developers, delivering performance close to its top-tier Claude Fable 5 on many tasks **at half the price** ($5/$25 per M tokens, identical to the prior Opus 4.8). It becomes the default model for Claude Max subscribers and is available across all paid plans and the Claude API.

## Key Features

- **Approaches Fable 5** capability across many tasks at half Fable's cost.
- **Effort dial** — users choose how much compute the model devotes to a task; at lower effort Opus 5 preserves much of its performance while using fewer tokens and costing less.
- **Mid-task model switching** — Anthropic launched an option to swap models mid-task to control cost.
- **Most aligned Opus model to date** — "least susceptible to being tricked into misuse," per Anthropic.
- **1M-token context window.**

## Cost / Quality Tradeoff

At $5/$25, Opus 5 is the same price as Opus 4.8 (quality 95) but a full generation newer and meaningfully more capable. It lands just below Claude Fable 5 (Anthropic's most powerful model, reserved for the most complex long-running autonomous tasks) and well above Sonnet-tier models. For heavy long-horizon agentic work, Fable 5 remains the go-to; for everyday frontier coding/reasoning, Opus 5 is the new default.
